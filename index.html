Chengzhi Hou (侯承志)

Doctoral Researcher · Combined Master’s–Ph.D. Program

School of Geography and Ocean Science, Nanjing University

Welcome to my academic website. I am a doctoral researcher at Nanjing University, China. My research focuses on the spatial and temporal variability of near-surface winds, with an emphasis on wind climatology, annual cycles, atmospheric circulation and long-term climate variability.

I also investigate how the temporal resolution of wind data affects wind energy resource assessment and aeolian transport estimates. My work combines surface observations, atmospheric reanalysis datasets and climate-model simulations.

Graduate studies at Nanjing University since 2021 · Expected Ph.D. completion: December 2027.
