01 / Profile

About me

I am a PhD candidate at the School of Geography and Ocean Science, Nanjing University, China. My research focuses on near-surface wind climatology and its applications to wind energy assessment and aeolian processes.

I investigate the spatial and temporal variability of near-surface winds across multiple timescales, the evaluation of atmospheric reanalysis datasets, and the effects of temporal resolution on wind power density and aeolian transport estimates.

My work draws on surface meteorological observations, atmospheric reanalysis products, and climate model simulations to examine wind-climate patterns and their environmental implications.
