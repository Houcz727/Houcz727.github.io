Research

Wind climate across timescales, with applications to wind energy resources and aeolian environments.

1. Near-surface Wind Climatology

My research characterizes spatial patterns and temporal variability of near-surface wind speed over land, from diurnal and annual cycles to interannual and decadal changes. I combine surface observations and reanalysis products to examine the geographical organization of wind climates and their links with atmospheric circulation and lower-tropospheric conditions.

Key topics: Wind annual-cycle regimes; spatial wind climatology; atmospheric circulation; climate variability.

2. Wind Energy Assessment

Wind power density depends nonlinearly on wind speed. I study the effects of averaging and temporal resolution on wind-resource estimates, using station observations, reanalysis products and climate-model data. My published work quantifies and calibrates large temporal-resolution-related biases in global onshore wind-power assessments.

Selected publication: Nature Communications (2025) ↗

3. Aeolian Processes and Wind Erosion

I investigate how wind variability and temporal averaging affect drift potential and wind-driven sediment transport, with a particular interest in deserts and dune systems in northern China. I also study wind-climate controls on long-term changes in dune activity.

Selected publication: Geophysical Research Letters (2025) ↗
